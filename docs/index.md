# Sillah (صلة) Family Health Management System

Sillah is a preventive health platform focused on reducing hereditary heart
disease risk in Saudi families through early detection, structured family
history tracking, and timely clinical action. This site documents its full
requirements lifecycle for SE311 Software Requirements Engineering at Prince
Sultan University.
