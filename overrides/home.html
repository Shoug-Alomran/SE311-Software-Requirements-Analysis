00 · Abstract

Most cardiac risk is visible years before the emergency.

Inherited heart conditions are often identified late. The signals are usually there already, in a parent's diagnosis or a grandparent's age at first event, but they are spread across people, memories and paper records.

Sillah gives families one place to record that history, applies rule-based risk checks across generations, and moves people from an alert to a screening appointment with a provider.

This project delivers the requirements baseline for that system: 30 testable functional requirements, a domain model, use cases, and a prioritised release scope, all traced to stakeholder needs.

01 · Prevention design

Four ideas shape every requirement.

Each requirement traces back to at least one of these prevention goals.

focus / 01

Early risk identification

Capture and structure family history so inherited risk patterns surface as early as possible, before the first cardiac event.

focus / 02

Preventive guidance

Clear recommendations and reminders that encourage regular screening and healthier habits.

focus / 03

Clinical readiness

Appointment workflows that move a family from alert to provider action quickly.

focus / 04

Governance & trust

Traceability, validation and change control keep health-related requirements reliable as the system evolves.
